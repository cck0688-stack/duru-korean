# "food" = the food-proverb layout for every Everything Else sheet

Sample: 음식 속담 읽기 (resource 91913a70-a176-488b-bdc5-acac706d6c48), made with
`redo-sheet.mjs --mode=proverb` (workflow redo-sheet.yml, mode `proverb`).

- Item section: one big rounded box; numbered disc, term bold with the gloss in
  brackets beside it, meaning on its own line, "예:" on its own line, thin dividers (`stack: true`).
- Conversation: turns (who + Korean) inside ONE big rounded box, no lines between turns (`dialogue` in the section).
- Reading tips (or the last text section): on page two (`newPage`), numbered points, note bar below.
- Your turn: green header box, boxed questions, "A: / B:" and (a)(b) parts on separate lines, ANSWERS box.
- Exactly two A4 pages; cut text, never shrink type. Type and sizes are the engine defaults.

When the owner says "food": redo every etc sheet this way (English first unless he says otherwise),
dry run, check the pictures, hold the replaced files (review) — never publish. Only etc sheets; change nothing else.
