한글 Curriculum

Learn Korean

A clear path from Hangul to real conversation.

한국어 학습 과정

Every level builds directly on the one before it — so you always know exactly what to study next.

Beginner

초급 · Level 1
Unit 1

Hangul: Vowels & Consonants

Read and write all 24 letters with correct stroke order.

Unit 2

Greetings & Introductions

Say hello, introduce yourself, and ask simple questions.

Unit 3

Sentence Structure Basics

Understand Korean word order and essential particles.

Unit 4

Numbers & Counting

Master both number systems and when to use each.

Unit 5

Everyday Vocabulary

Food, places, family, and other high-frequency words.

Unit 6

Present Tense & Basic Verbs

Form simple sentences to describe daily actions.

Intermediate

중급 · Level 2
Unit 1

Past & Future Tense

Talk about what happened and what's coming up.

Unit 2

Honorifics & Speech Levels

Speak appropriately with friends, elders, and strangers.

Unit 3

Connecting Sentences

Combine ideas naturally with common connectors.

Unit 4

Making Plans & Requests

Suggest, ask for favors, and negotiate politely.

Unit 5

Describing People & Things

Use adjectives and comparisons with confidence.

Unit 6

Travel & Daily Situations

Navigate transportation, shopping, and appointments.

Real Conversations

실전 회화 · Level 3
Unit 1

Natural, Casual Speech

Sound like a local with everyday spoken patterns.

Unit 2

Opinions & Small Talk

Share thoughts and hold a real back-and-forth conversation.

Unit 3

Listening to Native Speed

Train your ear with unscripted, real-life audio.
