About

Korean, made to reach you wherever you are.

두루 한국어 이야기

Duru (두루) means "widely, all around" — the idea that good Korean lessons shouldn't depend on where you live or how you learn best.

두
Our Story

Built for learners who never had the right guide.

Duru Korean started from a simple frustration: most Korean learning material was either too academic, too scattered, or too disconnected from how the language actually sounds. We wanted one clear, calm place — structured lessons, real audio, and no unnecessary complexity — so learners anywhere could make consistent progress.

Every lesson, chart, and page on this site is built around one goal: helping you go from your very first Hangul letter to speaking real, everyday Korean with confidence.
